AVALANCHE!!! But it caught the men you were chasing

Brooklyn sprinted down the mountainside as the avalanche roared behind her, a wall of white swallowing everything in its path. The men she’d been chasing weren’t fast enough; she saw them stumble, then vanish into the churning snow as the slope collapsed beneath them. The ground shook, icy wind tearing at her face, but she didn’t stop. She couldn’t. With the thunder of the avalanche at her back and the cold biting into her lungs, Brooklyn kept running, the only thought in her mind that she had to make it out — because whatever they’d stolen, whatever she was after, the danger wasn’t over just because the mountain had taken them.
Brooklyn didn’t stop running until the roar of the avalanche faded behind her, replaced by the sharp sting of cold air in her lungs. She knew she couldn’t handle this alone anymore — not after what she’d seen, not after what the men had been carrying before the mountain swallowed them. As soon as she reached the base of the trail, she headed straight for the nearest town, boots crunching through the snow, her mind replaying everything in frantic flashes. By the time she pushed open the door of the small police station, her hands were shaking, not from the cold but from the weight of what she was about to report. She wasn’t sure if they would believe her, but she knew one thing for certain: she had to tell someone before the danger caught up to her next.
