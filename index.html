The Adventure Begins


Brooklyn sat at the dining room table, absentmindedly stirring her cereal as she watched the clock creep closer to school time. That’s when she noticed the parcel. It was wrapped in thick brown paper and addressed to her in bold, familiar handwriting. Curious, she opened it carefully. Inside was a large leatherbound notebook, worn at the edges as if it had travelled thousands of kilometres. When she opened it, she found page after page of detailed maps, hand-drawn sketches of unusual places, and puzzles covered in strange symbols and coded messages from all over the world.
As she turned another page, a small note slipped out, attached to a debit card with her name on it. Her breath caught when she saw the signature—Uncle George, the explorer who was always chasing the next great discovery. The note was short but urgent. He was asking her to join him, saying he needed her help on his greatest adventure yet. Brooklyn looked from the notebook to the card, her mind racing with excitement and questions. Her mind raced and she decided to take the notebook to school to determine where she should begin.
