Mountie has seen your uncle

Brooklyn stood beside the Mountie in the middle of the thick forest, her boots sinking slightly into the damp ground as the chilly wind swept through the pine trees. The Mountie lifted his arm and pointed toward a narrow trail disappearing into the shadows. “That’s the direction I saw your uncle heading,” he explained, his voice calm but serious. “He looked like he was travelling fast, like he didn’t want to be followed.” Brooklyn stared down the path, her stomach twisting with worry and curiosity. After months without hearing from her uncle, she could hardly believe someone had finally seen him.

The Mountie glanced back at her before continuing. “From what I could tell, he was either trying to make his way toward Quebec or heading west toward Banff.” Brooklyn’s eyes widened as she imagined the long journey her uncle could be making through the wilderness. The forest around them suddenly felt mysterious and dangerous, every snapping twig making her jump. She pulled her jacket tighter around herself, knowing that wherever her uncle was going, there had to be a reason he was hiding
