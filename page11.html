At the Louvre

You study the latest clue in your uncle’s notebook, tracing the faded ink with your thumb.
The sketch looks like a pyramid, but the note beside it mentions “the great museum of secrets.”
You turn the page, hoping for more, but the rest is smudged beyond recognition.
Still, something about the drawing pulls at your memory — a shape you’ve seen before, somewhere grand, somewhere important.
Your heart beats faster as you realise the clue could point to two very different places, and only one will lead you closer to your missing uncle.

You step out into the bustling Paris street, the notebook tucked safely under your arm.
Ahead, the glass pyramid of the Louvre gleams in the sunlight, its sharp edges matching the sketch almost perfectly.
But another thought nags at you: what if the clue really does mean an actual pyramid — the kind buried in the sands of Egypt?
The decision weighs heavily on you. One path keeps you in Paris, the other sends you across continents.
Either way, your adventure is about to take a dramatic turn.
