Someone steals notebook

Once upon a time, there was a teenage girl named Brooklen who loved to read and learn new things. One day, she found a special book in her school library that contained important information about a secret project that could help her town. Excited and careful, Brooklen wrote down some notes in her own notebook to remember the details.



But one afternoon, while she was at the park with her friends, her notebook suddenly went missing. Brooklen looked everywhere, but her notebook was gone. She soon realized that somebody had stolen it! Her heart raced because she knew that if the wrong person got hold of her notes, they could cause a lot of trouble. She quickly glanced at the car and see someone holding here notebook in the car.
