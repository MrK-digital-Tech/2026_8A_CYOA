Statues right hand

Brooklyn steadied herself on the outstretched right hand of Christ the Redeemer, the wind tugging at her clothes as Rio stretched endlessly below. Her pulse hammered with a mix of fear and determination. She had chased clues across the city for days, each one colder than the last, until desperation pushed her to climb higher than she ever thought possible. From this dizzying height, the world felt impossibly vast—yet somehow, in this moment, it finally offered her an answer.

Then she saw him. Her uncle stepped into a yellow taxi far below, a worn leather bag slung over his shoulder and a plane ticket in his hand—France, unmistakable even from a distance. The sight jolted her, shrinking the city and sharpening her purpose. She had found him at last, but he was already slipping away again.
