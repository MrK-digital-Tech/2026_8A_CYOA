Showing notebook

Brooklyn stood just outside the school gate, sunlight catching the edges of the leather notebook she’d been delivered that morning. Her uncle’s parcel had felt mysterious from the moment she opened it, and now she proudly showed the notebook to her friends—Ken, Harmonie, and Barbie—each of them leaning in to admire the worn cover and the initials pressed into the corner. Ken wrapped an arm around Brooklyn’s waist, smilingat how excited she was, while Harmonie listened closely to every detail. But Barbie’s blue eyes lingered too long, her expression tightening with a strange mix of envy and curiosity that Brooklyn didn’t notice until it was too late.

In one swift motion, Barbie snatched the notebook from Brooklyn’s hands and stepped back with a smug grin, flipping through the pages as if she owned them. Brooklyn gasped, reaching out, but Barbie only held it tighter. Ken and Harmonie froze, shock washing over them as they watched their girlfriend’s trust shatter right in front of them. Harmonie’s mouth fell open, and Ken’s face hardened—his shock turning into anger. The betrayal was so blatant, so cruel, that Ken’s first instinct wasn’t just to demand the notebook back. He wanted justice. He wanted consequences. He wanted to file a lawsuit against Barbie for stealing something so personal and precious from Brooklyn, and nothing in his expression suggested he was joking as his eyes were turning red. He could only muster one last sentence to Barbie saying in a deep voice "When my eyes turn red run". Brooklyn had no time to deal with ken so she picked up the nearest object, which was a broken pipe and hit ken over the head with it, Ken was just a side character he always had been.
