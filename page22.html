Walking to the pyramids

Brooklyn had barely stepped off the plane before the heat of Egypt wrapped around her like a living thing, humming with history and possibility. The air tasted of sand and sun as she made her way toward the horizon, where the pyramids rose like ancient giants carved from gold. Each step she took stirred up little clouds of dust that clung to her white Nike Air Forces, but she didn’t mind; she felt as if she were walking straight into a story she’d been meant to live. As she approached the plateau, the Great Sphinx emerged from the shimmering light, its silent gaze fixed on her as though it already knew her name. Brooklyn slowed, her heart thudding with a mix of awe and anticipation.

Ahead of her, the pyramids towered in their impossible geometry, promising secrets buried deep within their stone corridors. To her right, the Sphinx waited, mysterious and inviting, its shadow stretching long across the sand. She paused between them, feeling the weight of the choice settle over her. Should she step into the cool, echoing chambers of the pyramids, where pharaohs slept and legends were born, or walk into the Sphinx itself, following the path of riddles and forgotten guardians? The desert wind brushed past her as if urging her forward, but only she could decide which ancient doorway would claim her first.
