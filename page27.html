Chase Through Hawaii

Brooklyn was walking across the sun-baked sand of Hawaii looking for a sign of anything suspicious then she sees it the man in the black suit she yells "hey I think that diary belongs to me!" he immediately bolts away. Brooklyn follows she goes jumping, ducking and weaving through fallen logs and vines when all of a sudden the jungle clears she sees a jeep driving away with the mans head sticking out the side staring at her shes about to give up.

But then she sees a bike coming towards her its flicking up dust everywhere thats when she realizes its actually a stark-vark one of the fastest e-bikes money can buy she pulls of a James bond back flip into an air dash than she pulls out a home made grapple hook and pulls the man of the stark she ride as fast as possible ignoring the man screaming in pain behind her she continues riding until she comes to a petrol station when she sees the jeep she gets closer and realizes nobodys guarding it she also sees the diary she could grab it and follow it to the treaser or she could follow the men back to there base.
