Man in a Black Car

Brooklynm stepped onto the school grounds clutching her uncle’s worn leather notebook, its pages stuffed with sketches, codes, and strange symbols she didn’t understand. She gathered her friends beneath the big jacaranda tree and flipped it open, showing them the odd diagrams and half‑finished maps. They laughed at first, teasing her about her “mystery treasure,” but the mood shifted when a sleek black car rolled to a slow stop beside the curb. A man in dark glasses stepped out, strode toward them without a word, and snatched the notebook from her hands before speeding away, leaving nothing but the smell of exhaust and a knot of fear tightening in her chest.

Her friends urged her to call the police, insisting that adults needed to handle something this serious. But Brooklynm remembered her uncle’s last message—cryptic, rushed, and ending with a warning to meet him at the airport if anything went wrong. Now she stood at a crossroads, heart pounding, knowing she had only minutes to choose. One path led to safety and official help; the other pulled her deeper into whatever secret her uncle had been tangled in. The bell rang, echoing across the courtyard, but Brooklynm didn’t move.
