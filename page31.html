A Secret Panel in the Wall


Patricia closed the hotel room door behind her and immediately began searching. The room looked ordinary at first—neatly made bed, wooden furniture, and a view of snow-covered mountains—but she knew her uncle wouldn’t have stayed somewhere without leaving a clue. She checked drawers, flipped through the hotel notepad, and even looked under the mattress, but found nothing. Just as she was about to give up, something caught her eye—a painting on the wall that hung slightly crooked. She stepped closer and carefully lifted it, revealing a small, hidden panel built into the wall behind it. Her pulse quickened. This had to be it.
She ran her fingers along the edges of the panel, searching for a latch or handle, but there was nothing—just a smooth surface sealed tightly shut. Patricia stepped back, thinking. She could force it open, but that might damage whatever was inside… or attract unwanted attention. Alternatively, she could look for a tool—something like a screwdriver—to pry it open more carefully. She glanced around the room again, weighing her options. Time was slipping away, and whatever her uncle had hidden behind that panel could be the key to everything.
