A Man Steals the Notebook


Brooklyn paddled hard, the canoe cutting through the calm water as the pontoon came closer with every stroke. The leather notebook rested beside her, its worn cover catching the sunlight. As she reached the edge, she steadied the canoe and reached for the rope to tie it off—but before she could react, a man crouched on the pontoon leaned down and grabbed the notebook straight from her hands. Brooklyn gasped, instinctively reaching after it, but he was already pulling it away, his grip firm and deliberate.
She looked up in shock as he turned and sprinted down the length of the pontoon. At the far end, a bicycle lay waiting, propped against the wooden boards as if planned in advance. Beyond the shoreline, she could see a taxi driving along the road, moving steadily past the scene, completely unaware of what had just happened. Brooklyn scrambled to climb out of the canoe, her mind racing—should she chase him onto the pontoon and grab the bike, or run for the road and try to catch the taxi before it disappeared?
