Sees Man With Uncles Hat

Brooklyn stood in the middle of the bustling street in New Delhi, still trying to make sense of how she’d ended up there. The air was thick with the scent of spices and car exhaust, and the sound of horns echoed through the chaos. She had been searching for the Arc de Triomphe—the one in Paris—but somehow, her adventure had led her to the Arc de Triomphe in India, a monument that looked eerily similar yet completely different. Confused but intrigued, she wandered closer, her eyes scanning the crowd for something familiar to anchor her in this strange twist of fate.
That’s when she saw him—a man standing near the arch, his back turned, wearing a hat that made her heart skip. It was her uncle’s hat, the same worn brown one with the frayed brim she’d seen countless times back home. Brooklyn froze, the noise of the city fading around her. Could it really be him? Or was this just another coincidence in her whirlwind journey? She stepped forward, calling out, her voice trembling with disbelief. In that moment, surrounded by the hum of a foreign city and the towering monument she hadn’t meant to find, Brooklyn realized her adventure had taken her somewhere far more mysterious than she ever imagined.
