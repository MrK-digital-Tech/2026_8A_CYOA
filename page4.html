Plane to Princeville

Brooklyn sat stiffly in her window seat as the plane droned toward Princeville, her fingers curled tight around the strap of her backpack. Two days ago, a plain brown package had vanished from her doorstep—snatched by a group of men in black who clearly thought she wouldn’t chase them. They were wrong. Through the glass, the ocean spread out like a sheet of blue fire, and Brooklyn felt her determination sharpen. Whatever they wanted with that package, it belonged to her. And the moment this plane touched down, she was going to find them, take it back, and make sure they regretted ever crossing her.
Brooklyn stood on the edge of Princeville’s quiet streets, the warm breeze brushing her face as her phone buzzed with the latest update. The thieves hadn’t stayed on the island—they were already heading toward Hawaii’s main island, putting more distance between her and the stolen package. Her stomach twisted. She could follow them, keep pushing forward on her own… or she could change course entirely and fly to Canada, where her uncle had the resources to help her track them properly. The choice loomed over her like a fork in the road: chase the thieves now while the trail was hot, or regroup with someone she trusted before diving deeper into danger.
