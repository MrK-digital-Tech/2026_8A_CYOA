Las Vegas

Brooklyn wandered through the electric haze of Las Vegas with the same restless energy that had carried her out of New Delhi, her white Nike Air Forces tapping against the pavement as if keeping time with the city’s heartbeat. The Strip stretched before her like a glowing river, every sign shouting in neon, every casino spilling laughter, music, and the clatter of slot machines into the warm desert air. She passed towering hotels shaped like pyramids and castles, each one trying to outdo the last, but none of them could distract her from the tug of her next adventure. The scents of street food, perfume, and cigarette smoke swirled together as crowds surged around her, yet she felt strangely calm, as though she were walking through a dream she’d already seen.

After days of weaving through the chaos of New Delhi’s markets and now the glittering spectacle of Vegas, she found herself craving a different kind of magic—one built on imagination rather than illusion. She stopped at an overlook where the city lights shimmered like a galaxy scattered across the desert floor, and for a moment she simply breathed it in. Ahead of her lay a choice that felt bigger than a plane ticket: should she head west to Disneyland in California, the original kingdom where every corner whispered nostalgia, or fly east to Florida, where Disney World sprawled like an entire world of its own, vast enough to get lost in for days? The decision hovered in the air around her, bright and tempting, as if the city itself were waiting to see which dream she would chase next.
