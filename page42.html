The Puzzle

Mermaid - Sade

🎝

The darkness was overwhelming until Brooklyn saw a wall ahead that seemed blank except for a small square carved in the center. It had two semicircles on each side with a circular dip inside. Brooklyn raised her finger and traced the engravings; imagining she was the mystery individuals who carved it.
As she traced, a semicircle moved, scraping roughly against the stone. She froze. What could this mean? Brooklyn recoiled and instictively bit her nails. She took a step forward. If one moved, so would the other.

But what side should she take?

Left or Right?
