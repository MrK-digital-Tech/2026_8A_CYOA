Note from your Uncle telling you to meet him in New Zealand

The letter felt heavier than paper should as the girl read it again, letting each word from Uncle George sink in. He wrote about how much he missed her, how the quiet hills of New Zealand weren’t the same without someone young and curious to share them with. He described the way the morning fog curled over the valleys like a soft blanket, the sound of sheep calling across the fields, and the little wooden house he’d fixed up just for her visit. As she held the letter, her heart thudded with a mix of excitement and nerves — traveling across the world felt enormous, almost unreal.
Yet the thought of seeing him again, of stepping into a place she’d only heard about in stories, filled her with a warm, hopeful ache. She imagined the long flight, the moment the plane doors opened, and Uncle George waiting with that familiar grin. For the first time in a long while, the future felt wide open, like a doorway she was finally brave enough to walk through..
