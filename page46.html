Volcano Starts to Erupt

Brooklyn stood on the trembling ridge, the sky bruised with ash as the volcano growled beneath her feet. Heat shimmered in the air, and every instinct screamed at her to run for the base where others were already fleeing. But above her, the distant thump of helicopter blades cut through the chaos, promising a faster escape—if it arrived in time. She weighed the two paths in a heartbeat, fear tightening her chest. Stay and risk the helicopter never reaching her, or run and risk being overtaken by the lava racing down the mountain. Either choice felt like stepping into fire, and the clock was already burning down.
