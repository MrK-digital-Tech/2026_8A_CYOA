Security catch you

Brooklyn quickly slipped her phone into her pocket after taking the photo of the Mona Lisa. The second she lowered her arm, two security guards stormed toward her through the crowded museum. Her pulse pounded in her ears as they began speaking sharply in French, demanding to see her phone. Brooklyn forced herself to stay calm, even though her mind was racing. She knew she could not tell anyone what she had noticed near the frame of the painting. Not the guards, not the tourists watching, not even the police if they arrived. Whatever she had seen was too important and too dangerous to speak about out loud.

One of the guards pointed toward a back office while another spoke into a radio, probably calling the police. Brooklyn swallowed hard and glanced toward the giant glass entrance of the museum. Outside, the streets of Paris buzzed with taxis and rushing crowds, and somewhere far beyond them was the airport. She could try to escape before the police arrived and make it onto the next flight out of France, or she could stay and attempt to explain herself in French. As the guards moved closer, Brooklyn’s mind spun with fear as she wondered which choice she should really make.
