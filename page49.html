MESSAGE IN THE NOTEBOOK: NORTH OR SOUTH?


Brooklyn stepped back from the Mona Lisa, her notebook still open in her hands. She had copied the strange symbol she’d seen hidden in the corner of the painting — a swirl followed by two sharp lines — but now she wasn’t looking at the artwork anymore. She was staring at the notebook. Something was wrong with the page. The symbol she had drawn was still there, but underneath it — appearing as if the paper itself had whispered it into existence — were three words she definitely hadn’t written. Her breath caught and a look of confusion cross her face.
The handwriting wasn’t hers. The ink was darker than her pencil. And the message was simple, but chilling: North or South Brooklyn blinked hard, thinking maybe she was imagining it. She rubbed her thumb across the words. The ink didn’t smudge. It was real. It was in the paper, like it had been waiting for her to find it. She flipped to the previous page. Blank. She flipped forward and backwards. Blank. She held the notebook up to the light. Nothing hidden. No impressions. No marks. Just that one message. North or South.
