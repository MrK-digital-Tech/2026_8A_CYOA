A Mysterious Car with Her Uncle Inside

The girl stood on the curb, squinting through the late‑afternoon glare as she spotted her uncle in the driver’s seat of a parked car. Another man sat beside him, the two of them talking in low, unreadable tones. She couldn’t hear a word, but something about the way they leaned in—serious, focused—made her pause. Her hand tightened around the strap of her bag as she tried to make sense of the scene, wondering why her uncle looked so different from the man she thought she knew.

The girl skidded to a stop at the corner just in time to see the car pull away, her uncle’s silhouette shrinking behind the tinted glass. Her bike waited beside her, pedals ready, but a taxi idled only a few metres ahead, its driver watching her with raised brows. Her pulse hammered as she weighed the choices—speed she controlled or speed she borrowed—before the car’s taillights flickered around the bend and forced her to decide.
