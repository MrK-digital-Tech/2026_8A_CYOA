CYOA

Brooklyn practically buzzed with energy as she stepped out into a day packed with possibilities, the kind that makes a teenage girl feel like the whole world is hers to play with. She bounced between ideas—maybe hitting the skate park with her friends, or wandering through the local shops hunting for cute accessories, or even grabbing her camera to explore hidden corners of town for the perfect aesthetic shots. If the sun stayed warm, she’d probably end up at the pool, splashing around and laughing until her cheeks hurt. And if the weather turned, no problem—she had a stack of art supplies, a playlist ready to blast, and a dozen half-finished creative projects waiting for her attention. For Brooklyn, fun wasn’t something she had to look for; it followed her everywhere she went.
