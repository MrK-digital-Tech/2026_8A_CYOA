Deeper into the Pyramid


Brooklyn stepped cautiously into the chamber, her footsteps echoing softly against the ancient stone walls. The air was cool and still, carrying a sense of age and mystery. As her eyes adjusted to the dim light, she noticed something sitting in the centre of the room—a large, ornately carved chest. Its surface was covered in intricate patterns and symbols, worn but still clear enough to suggest it had been placed there with purpose. She moved closer, her curiosity growing, knowing this could be the clue she had been searching for.
She paused beside the chest, glancing back toward the narrow passage she had just come through. Opening it here might reveal something important straight away—but it could also trigger something she wasn’t prepared for. On the other hand, dragging it outside into the open would be safer, but slower and far more difficult. Brooklyn rested her hand on the lid, thinking carefully. Would she risk opening it immediately, or take the safer option and move it before discovering what was inside?
