A Key, but to Where?


Brooklyn took a steady breath and focused on the final piece of the wall puzzle. The symbols had to align—but something about the pattern felt slightly off. She hesitated, then pushed the last stone piece to the left instead of into the centre. There was a sharp click, followed by a low grinding sound as the mechanism shifted inside the wall. A small compartment suddenly opened, and a metal key dropped out, clinking softly against the stone floor.
She picked it up and turned the attached tag over in her hand. New England. Her brow furrowed as she quickly flipped through her uncle’s notebook. He had mentioned New England before—but not clearly enough to make it easy. One reference pointed to the northeastern United States, while another hinted at the New England region in Australia. Brooklyn looked back at the key, realising this clue didn’t solve anything—it created a new decision.
