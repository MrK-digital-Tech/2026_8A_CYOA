room full of gas

Brooklyn stood in the middle of the dim room, her dark curly hair brushing her shoulders and her deep brown skin catching what little light remained. Her blue jeans and white shirt made her look like she’d just stepped in from an ordinary day—until the sharp hiss cut through the silence. She turned toward the sound, confusion tightening her brow as a thin stream of gas began to spill from a vent near the floor. The pale cloud curled around her shoes first, rising slowly, unnervingly, as the air grew heavy and chemical.

Her heartbeat quickened. Brooklyn took a step back, then another, her curls bouncing as she scanned the room for any sign of escape. The gas thickened, swirling like fog, brushing against her legs and climbing higher. The white fabric of her shirt fluttered with each shallow breath she forced herself to take. She pressed a hand to the wall, steadying herself, knowing she had only moments before the room filled completely. The ordinary space around her had transformed into something dangerous, and every instinct told her she needed to move—now.
