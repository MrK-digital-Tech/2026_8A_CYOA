Lost Treasure


Brooklyn stood on the wide Hawaiian beach, gripping the shovel tightly as she dug yet another hole in the sand. Around her, dozens of uneven holes marked where she had already searched, each one a reminder of her growing frustration. She was certain she had buried the treasure here—but everything looked different now. The tide had come in overnight, and heavy rain had washed away the patterns in the sand, the driftwood she had used as a marker, even the small rock formation she had carefully lined up. What had once been a clear plan now felt like a guessing game she was losing.
After another failed attempt, Brooklyn let the shovel fall beside her and slowly sat down in the sand. The sun was beginning to set, casting a golden glow across the water, but she barely noticed its beauty. Instead, she stared out at the horizon, replaying her decisions over and over in her mind. How could she have trusted something so temporary? The waves rolled in and out, as if quietly erasing the last traces of her mistake. For the first time, she wondered if the treasure was gone for good—and whether this was a lesson she would never forget.
