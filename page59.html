REWARDED

Brooklyn sank into her airplane seat with a grin she couldn’t hide, the soft hum of the engines matching the warm flutter in her chest. After losing her uncle’s leather notebook, she thought her journey was over—but instead, it had led her straight to a hidden cache of treasure he’d left behind for her to discover. Gold coins, old maps, and a letter praising her courage now filled her backpack, safely tucked beneath the seat. For the first time in days, she felt light, proud, and certain she’d done exactly what her uncle hoped she would. The clouds outside the window glowed orange as the plane lifted, and Brooklyn rested her head back, finally letting herself breathe.
