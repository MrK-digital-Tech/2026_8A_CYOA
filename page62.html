Kidnappers caught with Uncle in hideout

Brooklyn froze in shock as a dark van screeched to a halt outside the abandoned house. The kidnappers had finally found her uncle. Before he could react, two men rushed forward, grabbed him, and forced a black bag over his head. Her uncle struggled and shouted for help, but the kidnappers quickly dragged him towards the waiting vehicle. Hidden behind a broken fence, Brooklyn watched helplessly as the men bundled him into the back of the van.



The engine roared to life and the van sped away in a cloud of dust, disappearing down the deserted road. Brooklyn's heart pounded as she realised her uncle was gone. She pulled out her leatherbound notebook and scribbled down everything she had seen—the colour of the van, part of the number plate, and the direction it had travelled. Taking a deep breath, she knew there was only one thing to do. Somehow, she had to track down the kidnappers and rescue her uncle before it was too late.
