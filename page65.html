Ancient Ruins in Myanmarr

She had spent weeks searching through the dense jungles of Myanmar, following the faint trail her uncle had left behind. When she finally reached the ancient ruins, the air was thick with mystery — moss‑covered stones, carved faces watching from the shadows, and the faint echo of footsteps that weren’t hers. Then she saw him: her uncle, weary but alive, clutching the same faded map that had started it all. His eyes lit up when he saw her, but before they could speak, the sound of boots and shouted orders shattered the silence.

Uniformed officers emerged from the mist, surrounding them. Her uncle tried to explain, tried to show the map as proof of his research, but it was too late — they seized him, declaring his exploration illegal. As he was caught and deported, he turned back one last time, pressing a torn piece of the map into her hand. “Finish what I started,” he whispered. The ruins swallowed his voice, and she stood alone among the ancient stones, realizing the secret he’d uncovered was now hers to protect. wich one should she choose?
