Earthquake uncover hidden Maori Artifact. Give to Museum

Brooklyn had just flown over to New Zealand after receiving a mysterious note from her uncle, hoping it would finally lead her to him. She searched for clues across different towns and landscapes, but nothing seemed to point her in the right direction. Even though she felt unsure and a little lost, she kept going, determined not to give up on finding answers.

A few days later, a sudden earthquake tore through the area, shaking the ground beneath her feet and cracking open parts of the landscape. After the tremors stopped, Brooklyn carefully explored the damaged area and noticed something partly buried in the freshly broken earth. Hidden beneath layers of soil was an ancient Māori artefact unlike anything she had ever seen before. Realising how important and culturally valuable it was, Brooklyn protected the discovery and quickly brought it to the museum so experts could preserve it properly and learn more about its history.
