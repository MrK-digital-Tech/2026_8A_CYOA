Overcome from smoke

The ground trembles beneath your feet as you stare up at the towering volcano.
A deep, growling rumble echoes from inside the mountain, sending flocks of birds scattering into the sky.
Heat radiates through the air, and the smell of ash drifts on the wind.
Your uncle’s notebook warned that danger would follow the clues, but you never imagined you’d be
standing at the base of an active volcano, trying to decide your next move.
Every instinct tells you that time is running out.

You tighten your grip on the notebook and look toward the narrow path spiraling up the volcano’s side.
Maybe the height will give you a better view of where your uncle was taken. But another path curves around the base,
disappearing into the trees — safer, maybe, but slower, and you can’t shake the feeling that something important
might be hidden along the outer trail. The volcano rumbles again, louder this time,
forcing you to choose quickly before the mountain makes the choice for you.
Either way, your adventure is about to take a dramatic turn.
