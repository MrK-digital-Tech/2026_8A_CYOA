Brooklyn is flying


Brooklyn pressed her forehead to the airplane window, watching the clouds peel away as the plane dipped toward the rugged landscape below. Jagged peaks rose like ancient teeth, their shadows stretching across the valley in shapes she recognized instantly. One mountain in particular made her breath hitch—the same one sketched in the margins of her uncle’s old notebook, the one he’d labeled only with a cryptic phrase: “the place where it begins.” Now, seeing it from above, she felt a strange pull, as if the mountain itself were calling her name. The closer the plane drifted, the more certain she became that this wasn’t a coincidence.
As the aircraft banked, sunlight spilled across the lower slopes, revealing a dark slash at the mountain’s base—a cave, exactly where the notebook’s drawing had hinted. A chill rippled through her despite the recycled cabin air. The cave looked impossibly deep, a mouth carved into stone, waiting. Brooklyn’s pulse quickened as she realized her uncle hadn’t been scribbling stories or daydreams; he’d been leaving clues. And now, soaring above the world with nothing but her backpack and a racing heart, she understood that whatever mystery he’d been tangled in was about to become hers too. If she followed that cave, she might finally uncover the truth behind his secrets—and maybe the truth about herself.
