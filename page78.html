Chest

A teenage girl stood before an old chest, curiosity shimmering in her eyes. As she lifted the lid, a thick, strange gas poured out, swirling around her like a living mist. Within seconds, her vision blurred, and she collapsed—unconscious.
When she awoke, the sterile white of a hospital room surrounded her, machines humming softly beside her bed. Confused and weak, she pieced together fragments of memory—the chest, the gas, the sudden darkness. Days later, still haunted by the mystery, she boarded a plane to fly home, staring out the window as clouds drifted below, wondering what secrets that chest had truly held.
