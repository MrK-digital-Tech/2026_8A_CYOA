Everything felt alive around her — the sway of palm trees, the distant crash of waves, the hum of chatter from travelers and locals alike. Clutching her worn leather backpack, she unfolded the old map she’d found tucked inside her uncle’s journal. A red “X” marked a spot deep within the island’s lush interior, and beneath it, a single handwritten note: “Follow the path where the mountains breathe.”

With determination sparking in her chest, she set off along a narrow trail that wound through towering ferns and vibrant tropical flowers. The deeper she walked, the quieter the world became, until all she could hear was the rhythmic rustle of leaves and her own footsteps. The map guided her toward a rocky cliffside where a faint opening peeked through layers of vines.
