Brooklyn thinks back over the last two months she spent traveling across the world searching for her missing uncle. Now she had no idea where she was and just when she was about to give up hope, she finally spotted him walking into a huge amphitheatre filled with people. Brooklyn hurried after him, her heart pounding with excitement and confusion.

Curious, Brooklyn sat in the crowd and listened as he stepped onto the stage. As he spoke, Brooklyn realized he had been talking about her the entire time. By the end of the speech, the audience applauded loudly, and Brooklyn ran onto the stage.
