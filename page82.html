Fall and Break Leg

Brooklyn pushed open the old wooden door, not expecting the floor on the other side to dip so sharply. Her foot slipped, and before she could catch herself, she tumbled forward, landing hard on the cold ground. A sharp, searing pain shot through her leg, stealing her breath as she realized something was terribly wrong. The world tilted, her vision blurring at the edges, and all she could do was clutch her throbbing leg and hope someone heard her fall.
