A Room of Treasure!!

A rush of cool air swept past the girl as she launched herself down the smooth, twisting slide, her laughter echoing off the walls until the chute suddenly opened into a vast chamber shimmering with gold. She landed in a soft spill of coins, their metallic clink ringing through the treasure room like a welcome. Piles of glittering crowns, ancient goblets, and jewel‑studded chests rose around her in glowing mounds, each one catching the warm light that danced across the cavern. For a moment she simply stared, breathless, realizing she had slipped—quite literally—into a place most people only dreamed of discovering.
