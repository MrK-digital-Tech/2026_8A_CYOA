Oh no! A Camel in My Face


Brooklyn hesitated only for a moment before choosing a door, her instincts telling her it was the right way out. As soon as she stepped through, she heard a hiss behind her. The room she had just left began filling with gas, forcing her forward with no time to think. The ground suddenly dropped away beneath her feet, and she found herself sliding rapidly down a narrow chute hidden inside the pyramid. Twisting and turning in the darkness, she had no control as she was launched out the other end, flying into the bright sunlight and landing face first in the hot sand.
She groaned and rolled onto her side, spitting out grit, her adventure ending far less gracefully than she had imagined. As she lay there catching her breath, a curious camel wandered over and began licking her face, its rough tongue making her laugh despite everything. But the moment quickly faded as reality set in. Exhausted, sore, and frustrated, Brooklyn realised she had reached the end of this path with nothing to show for it. Not long after, she found herself on a flight back to Australia, staring out the window, feeling defeated—but quietly wondering where the next clue might lead if she ever got another chance.
