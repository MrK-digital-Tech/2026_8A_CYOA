Brooklyn stood in the center of the airport walkway, her heart pounding harder than the footsteps rushing past her. The leather notebook her uncle had sent—the one thing that was supposed to guide her—was gone, stolen somewhere between the chaos of her journey and her own frantic decisions. Now she had nothing but a pamphlet she’d picked up off the floor, its glossy cover showing two destinations: Banff and Quebec. But what made her freeze wasn’t the travel options—it was the familiar face printed on the back. Ken. Her classmate. The same Ken she had accidentally knocked out at the start of all this. His photo smiled back at her like nothing had happened, like he wasn’t tangled in the mess she’d created. But Brooklyn didn’t have time to unravel that mystery. She had to choose where to go next if she wanted any chance of finding her uncle—or the missing notebook.

She made her way to the paying station, rehearsing her choices under her breath. Banff for wilderness and quiet clues… Quebec for crowds and hidden trails… Her palms were sweaty, her mind racing, and she was seconds away from choosing when a sudden commotion erupted behind her. Two border security officers marched through the terminal, and between them—looking dazed, confused, and very much like someone who had no idea why this was happening—was Ken. Brooklyn’s jaw dropped as she watched him being escorted away, hands raised in bewilderment. People stared. Someone laughed. And Ken, poor Ken, looked like he’d just woken up in the wrong universe. Brooklyn’s stomach twisted. She wanted to run after him, to explain, to apologize—but the clerk was already calling her forward. Her decision couldn’t wait, even as Ken disappeared behind a security door never to be seen again.
